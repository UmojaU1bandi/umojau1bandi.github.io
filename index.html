KUHUSU SISIABOUT US

UMOJA-U1 BANDI COMPANY LTD UMOJA-U1 BANDI COMPANY LTD

Sisi ni wazalishaji wa Tanzania Breadcrumbs, jijini Dar es Salaam. Bidhaa zetu hutumika kwenye mapishi mbalimbali ya nyumbani na biashara — ikiwemo harusi, vikao, mikutano na hoteli. We produce Tanzania Breadcrumbs in Dar es Salaam. Our products are used in home and business cooking — including weddings, meetings, gatherings and hotels.

Ni chembechembe za mkate halisi uliookwa na kukaushwa kwa joto la juu ili kupata ukavu na wepesi wa kipekee — 100% asilia, hazina rangi za bandia wala vihifadhi vya kemikali. Made from real baked bread, dried at high heat for a light, crisp texture — 100% natural, with no artificial colors or chemical preservatives.

Zinapatikana katika aina mbili — White na Brown — na ujazo wa 250g, 400g na 1kg. Pia tunauza Jilambe Pilau Masala, kiungo cha 4 katika 1 (kitunguu saumu, mdalasini, hiliki, karafuu). Available in two types — white and brown — in 250g, 400g and 1kg sizes. We also sell Jilambe Pilau Masala, a 4-in-1 spice blend (garlic, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves).

JINSI YA KUTUMIAHOW TO USE

Hatua 3 rahisi3 simple steps

1

Paka bidhaa yako (kuku/samaki) kwenye unga wa ngano au yai lililopigwa.Coat your chicken or fish in flour or beaten egg.

2

Izungushe kwenye Tanzania Breadcrumbs mpaka ifunikwe vizuri.Roll it in Tanzania Breadcrumbs until fully coated.

3
